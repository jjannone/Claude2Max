### Audio spectrum and waveform delivered to shaders as textures (MadMapper)

- **Max:** Partly in Max · confidence medium · unreviewed
- **Theme:** Shader authoring conveniences
- **There:** `audioFFT`, `audio`, `SIZE`, `ATTACK`, `DECAY`, `RELEASE`, `Audio Analysis Channels`
- **What it does:** A shader input declared as spectrum or waveform receives a one-pixel-high texture of up to 512 values, with attack, decay and release smoothing applied to each band before the shader sees it.
- **Closest in Max:** `jit.catch~`, `jit.gl.texture`
- **Checked:** Searched Max 9.1.5's object registry (obj-qlookup.json), refpage names, digests and text, the userguide topics, and the repo's package library (query_packages.py). jit.catch~'s refpage: turns MSP signals into a stream of matrices, which can be sent to a shader as a texture. The FFT and the per-band smoothing are patched separately.
- **Source:** <https://raw.githubusercontent.com/madmappersoftware/MadMapper-Materials/HEAD/MaterialsDoc.md> · <https://docs.madmapper.com/madmapper/6/3.-media/writing-custom-glsl-materials>
